Team

Not for sale yet

What it would be, if enough people want it:

  • Lock and watch servers that require credentials: yours, your vendors', your internal ones
  • Hosted nightly checks with no CI to maintain, and alerts where your team reads them
  • A private history page per server, with the full tool text of every version, not just the hash
  • Exportable evidence: "this is what the server served on this date", signed
  • Someone to call when a check says something you do not understand

Tell me you would pay

Why there is no credit-card form

Billing, accounts, hosted checks and alerting are infrastructure that has to be kept alive every day by someone. I run the crawler, the log and this site alone, as a side project, and I will not add a paid service I cannot stand behind. The free tier is complete and will stay complete: nothing on this site is a crippled version of something behind a paywall.

The Team plan opens when five teams have said, in writing, that they would pay for it. The counter below is the number of GitHub issues carrying the team-plan label. It is read live, and it is allowed to say zero.
