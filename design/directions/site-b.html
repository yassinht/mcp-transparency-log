About

The log started on 30 August 2026 as one question: is the MCP ecosystem observable at all, or is "trust the registry" the only option? It was. Eighty nights later it holds 2.2 million signed observations, and the official registry's maintainers used its measurements as evidence when they designed periodic re-validation of entries.

It is built and run by Yassine Houta, a software engineer in Tunisia, and delivered to companies with experteam.tn. The code is Apache-2.0. The design rules are simple and public: raw bytes are the record, every judgement is a rule a human can re-run, no model anywhere in the data path, and every correction we ever made to our own numbers is still in the README.

Other people watch the same servers. We publish our nightly observations in the format they read, because two independent observers that agree are worth more than one that asks to be believed.
