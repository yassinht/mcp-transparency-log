Why there is no credit-card form

Billing, accounts and hosted alerting are infrastructure that has to be kept alive every day by someone. The crawler, the log and this site are run by one engineer; experteam.tn handles contracts and invoices so that nothing paid depends on a service that cannot be stood behind. The free tier is complete and will stay complete: nothing on this site is a crippled version of something behind a paywall.
